title: MARCO-BOLO
description: Coastal and marine areas face intense pressures from overexploitation, climate change, pollution, and development, yet many monitoring programmes remain fragmented and short-term. MARCO-BOLO aims to transform marine biodiversity monitoring by connecting existing initiatives, improving methods, and innovating technologies. The project delivers practical tools that allow politicians and companies to assess biodiversity health, predict changes, and proactively manage environments.
