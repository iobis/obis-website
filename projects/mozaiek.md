title: MOZAIEK
description: MOZAIEK (2027–2029), funded by Flanders through FUST and led by IOC-OBIS with GOOS, will transform fragmented marine biodiversity observations into harmonised, quality-controlled, analysis-ready data packages linked to five GOOS EOVs: phytoplankton, fish, coral, seagrass and macroalgae. The project will deliver at least ten packages, open processing pipelines, data-gap analyses and training materials. A Caribbean demonstrator, developed with the GOOS Co-Design Programme and regional partners, will test their use in marine heatwave early warning. The infrastructure will also support conservation, fisheries management, marine spatial planning and biodiversity reporting.
