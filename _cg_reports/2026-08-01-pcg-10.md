## Introduction

The OBIS Products Coordination Group met to discuss statistics to support CBD reporting, a national data portal built on OBIS data, and progress on the Products Catalogue, and to look ahead to next year's work plan and the end of the current Co-Chairs' terms.

## Statistics for CBD reporting

The group talked through two initial solutions: statistics on each Node page showing records that are new or updated in the current year, and a new endpoint for querying download events (an example is available in the OBIS Metrics API: https://api.obis.org/#/Metrics). Statistics may need to be built around countries or IPT installations, in addition to Nodes. Whatever solutions are adopted, the plan is to educate Nodes on these tools in collaboration with the Nodes Coordination Group.

## CIOOS Data Explorer

Richard Kelly presented the CIOOS Data Explorer as an example of what a national portal could look like, built by scraping Canadian data from OBIS, including data from OBIS Canada and OTN. It could serve as a model for other Nodes or regions to adopt. A recording is available to members of the OBIS network.

## Products Catalogue

The group reviewed a new design direction for the catalogue and the governance draft, which is open for review. All comments on the catalogue governance are due by 10 September.

## Looking ahead

**Work plan.** The OBIS Work Plan is being developed, and there will be a request for rapid feedback ahead of the IODE meeting in October. The PCG's own work for next year will be designed at SG-OBIS-14 in November, and members are encouraged to think about what the group should work on and to contact the Co-Chairs with ideas.

**Co-Chair terms.** The current Co-Chairs' terms are coming to an end. Jon Pye has volunteered to stay on for one more year as a bridge, and Stephen Formel and Silas Principe will alternate in providing support through the Secretariat. The group is looking for a new Co-Chair to start in November, and a second Co-Chair to start in November 2027. A Co-Chair does not need to work for an OBIS Node. Anyone interested is asked to get in touch.
