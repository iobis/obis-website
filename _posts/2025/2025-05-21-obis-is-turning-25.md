OBIS is turning 25 years old! To celebrate this milestone, we asked the members of the OBIS Community, "What does OBIS mean to *you*?"
This page gathers their stories, memories, and wishes for the future. You want to contribute? Click on the "+" sign at the bottom of the board and share your message! You can upload photos, videos, sound, and texts. Your contribution will be visible once validated by the admins. Posts appear in chronological order (newest first).
