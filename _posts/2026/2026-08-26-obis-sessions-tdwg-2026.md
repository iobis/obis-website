Are you joining the TDWG 2026 conference in Oslo, Norway, on 21–25 September? We have rounded up, day by day, all the OBIS-related programme items. TDWG 2026 is touching upon numerous topics of interest to the OBIS community beyond the items listed below! You can access the full conference programme [here](https://www.tdwg.org/conferences/2026/program/schedule/). Are you part of the OBIS Community and participating in an agenda item we haven't listed? Send us a message at [helpdesk@obis.org](mailto:helpdesk@obis.org).
<br><br>

### Monday 21 September

14:00–16:00, Plenary (UiO Aula)<br>
*Extending Darwin Core to Represent Sociobiodiversity and Traditional Knowledge*, [Clara Baringo Fonseca](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/74311) (OBIS Brazil) — Talk in the Taster session.

### Tuesday 22 September

8:30–10:30, Sal B<br>
*Toward RASD Implementation in Brazil: Integrating Conservation, Open Data, and Traditional Knowledge Safeguards*, [Clara Baringo Fonseca](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/74311) (OBIS Brazil) — Talk in the Symposium: SYM23 Managing restricted access data (RASD).

14:00–15:30, Sal C<br>
*Modernizing Brazil's Taxonomic Backbone: How ChecklistBank Supports Biodiversity and Genetic Heritage Systems*, [Clara Baringo Fonseca](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/74311) (OBIS Brazil) — Talk in the Contributed Oral session: CO2 Digitizing Collections & Legacy Data.

16:00–17:00, Balder<br>
WM48 IQAIR Task Group Work Meeting, co-led by [Yasin Bakış](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/38966) and [Hank Bart](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/33848) (Fish OBIS).

### Thursday 24 September

8:30–10:30, Sal B<br>
SYM15 Operationalizing Biodiversity Digital Twins within Data Space Ecosystems — Symposium, co-led by [Dag Endresen](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/64359) (OBIS Norway).

11:00–12:30, Sal B<br>
*Challenges and Opportunities for FAIR Marine eDNA Data Publication Workflows*, [Berenice Talamantes Becerra](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/87846) (OBIS Australia) [virtual] — Talk in the Contributed Oral session: CO4 FAIR data in practice: eDNA, modelling and biodiversity concepts.

11:00–12:30, Sal C<br>
*Toward Quantitative AI-Readiness Metrics for Biodiversity Data Infrastructures*, [Yasin Bakış](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/38966) (Fish OBIS) — Talk and organiser of the Symposium: SYM46 AI-Readiness Metrics and Metadata for Biodiversity Data.

16:00–17:00, Forum<br>
DS49 What Makes Biodiversity Data "Robot-Ready"? Standards, Infrastructure, and AI — Discussion Session, co-organized by [Yasin Bakış](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/38966) and [Hank Bart](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/33848) (Fish OBIS).

### Friday 25 September

8:30–10:00 & 10:30–12:00, Sal A<br>
SYM47 Large Language Models for Biodiversity Data Discovery, Integration, and Curation — Symposium in two parts, co-organized by [Yasin Bakış](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/38966) (Fish OBIS).

10:30–12:00, Sal C<br>
*ASEAN Biodiversity Dashboard as a data-sharing platform to monitor biodiversity trends and species occurrences in the ASEAN Region*, [Christian "Kit" Elloran](https://oceanexpert.org/expert/21501) (SEA OBIS) — Talk and organiser role in the Symposium: SYM28 From Standards to Implementation: Connecting Observation Data in Asia to GBIF Infrastructure.
