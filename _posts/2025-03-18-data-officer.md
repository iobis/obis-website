OBIS seeks a skilled Data Officer to support the MARCO-BOLO project. Funded by the EU Horizon Europe programme (along with UKRI), MARCO-BOLO is a €7.3 million, 4-year project that will strengthen European marine, coastal and freshwater biodiversity observation to understand and restore ocean health.

This 9-month consultancy (April–December 2025\) is a unique opportunity to contribute to cutting-edge marine observation efforts in Europe and beyond. This is a remote position under contract with the OBIS Secretariat/IODE office in Oostende, Belgium.

Applications are open **until 25 March 2025, 17:00 CET**.  
Read all the application requirement details and application process [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hj68u7EKfZ1vDgDt_ne-yqgjkvNA9GwE/view?usp=sharing).
