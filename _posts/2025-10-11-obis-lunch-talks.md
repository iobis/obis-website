Join us at the OBIS booth during Living Data 2025 for a series of interactive 30-minute “Lunch Talks”\! Each day features two short sessions designed to help you discover, use, and contribute to OBIS. Whether you are new to OBIS or already an active contributor, these talks will help you make the most of OBIS tools, data, and community support\! 

Please note: The programme below is tentative and may be subject to changes.

## **21 October – Get Started with OBIS\!**

**Talk 1: Publish your first dataset to OBIS**  
 A practical introduction to the publishing workflow: who can contribute, what you need, and how to get your data online quickly.

**Talk 2: Find your data in a few clicks\!**  
 A live demonstration of OBIS search, filters, and mapping tools to explore marine biodiversity data.
 

## **22 October – A Deeper Dive into OBIS Data**

**Controlled vocabularies in your datasets**  
How standard vocabularies (e.g. life stage, sampling instruments, measurement types) ensure data are interoperable and discoverable. What is brewing on that front.

**Beyond the points on a map**  
How to explore OBIS data in more depth using eMoF, quality flags, advanced search filters, the OBIS mapper and more.


## **23 October – Turning Data into Insights**

**More data for your datasets**  
How contributors can enrich their datasets with multimedia to increase their discoverability and value.

**Make the most out of OBIS data: Using the OBIS Tools**  
Showcase of the OBIS mapper, species distribution maps, depth range visualizations, and other built-in tools, with quick use-case examples.


## **24 October – Harness the Power of the OBIS Community\!**

**Become an OBIS contributor: OBIS resources and trainings – we have got you covered\!**  
 A panorama of the available OBIS-related resources and training opportunities, including GitHub, Slack, and online manuals.

**Make the most out of OBIS data: The utmost importance of the OBIS Nodes**  
 Discover how the OBIS Node network is the backbone of community support, and how you can identify and connect with your regional Node for guidance on data publishing, standards, or using data regionally.

## 👋  Join Us at Booth \#3A\! You won’t be free at lunch time? No worries, come and see us at a coffee break. We have the coolest swag for you\!
