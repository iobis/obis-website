<h1><span class="usecase-icon-badge"><i class="bi bi-shield-exclamation" style="color: var(--tags);"></i></span>Invasive Alien Species &amp; Biosecurity</h1>

OBIS delivers early warning and monitoring for invasive threats that endanger biodiversity, fisheries, and national economies. By aggregating georeferenced occurrence data across ocean basins, OBIS provides the baseline needed to detect new introductions, reconstruct invasion histories, and assess risk at ports, shipping lanes, and aquaculture sites.

*Supports: KMGBF Target 6, CBD, IPBES IAS Assessment, IMO Ballast Water Convention*

<br>
